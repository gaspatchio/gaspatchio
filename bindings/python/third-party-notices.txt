Third-party software in gaspatchio's compiled extension

gaspatchio._internal, the extension module every gaspatchio wheel carries, is
compiled from this repository's two Rust crates, from the 287 crates below and
from the Rust standard library. The first are Opio Inc.'s work; this file
carries the notices of the rest. The crates are every crate Cargo.lock
resolves as a normal dependency of bindings/python on the platforms the wheels
are built for, x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu, x86_64-apple-darwin,
aarch64-apple-darwin and x86_64-pc-windows-msvc, and on
aarch64-pc-windows-msvc and aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu, where pip builds the
sdist for want of a wheel. A crate that only some of those platforms use is
listed once and says so. A few are compiled and then left out by the linker,
because no code calls them; they are listed all the same. Crates used only
while the extension is built are not in it and are not listed: build scripts,
procedural macros and what either uses, and the dependencies of tests. The
standard library, which the Rust toolchain supplies rather than Cargo.lock,
has a section of its own after the crates.

Each crate is listed with the licence it declares and the licence it is taken
under here: where it offers a choice, the first of MIT, Apache-2.0,
BSD-3-Clause, BSD-2-Clause, ISC, Zlib, Unicode-3.0, BSL-1.0 that it offers,
unless about.toml says otherwise and why. The numbers are the texts that
follow: 169 as the crates ship them, each with the crates it is carried for,
then each licence's standard text. A crate that ships no text of its own for
its licence cites the standard one. Where a crate's sources carry a notice of
their own, a licence written out at the head of a file under a copyright the
crate's licence files do not name, that notice is carried beside the crate's.

The Level 4 tutorial's data, which the wheel also carries, derives from
lifelib, under MIT; its attribution is in NOTICE, beside this file.

Written by scripts/rust_notices.py from Cargo.lock, with cargo-about 0.9.2
configured by about.toml; edits here are lost.
